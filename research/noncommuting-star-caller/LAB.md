five-leaf words have no two-map completion. The rank searches in
`rank_search.py` are finite. An unknown result is a timeout.

Ten full calls on52 blocks need1,404 rank-one thin calls. This word loses.
`replay_two_map_word.py` independently checks all576 entries with scalar
polynomial sets. It uses no prefix inverses or search imports. Normal and
optimized Python pass. Two omitted-map controls and one changed coefficient
control fail. `TWO-MAP-REPLAY.json` records the check.
No characteristic-zero or native lift is supplied. The total-rank-ten test
in `coupled-query-g5-gap14-r10.json` timed out.

## A common advancing stage

The proposed native stage applies an eight-axis Hadamard, then swaps two
eight-axis blocks. It has order four; its square has Gaussian rank16. The
full native source binding and phases remain unchecked. The floating prices
in `new-query-price-screens.json` are for unconstructed target profiles.

`cyclic_simplex.cpp` and `cyclic_pair_search.py` adapt pinned predecessors to
D^4=1. The four-field census has no endpoint. The six-field pair test is
unsatisfiable in its binary model. `cyclic_stage_search.py` also tests direct
sums and mixed six-type corank-two Gram frames. The two-triangle positive
control passes literal replay. Larger direct-sum searches timed out. The
mixed four-field frame is unsatisfiable in the formal balanced test and the
cyclic query test. The six-field balanced test timed out.

The shifted solver uses z=D+1. In the order-four case it imposes z^4=0.
Every positive result is replayed in D coordinates. The six-type Gram model
comes from the released2026-10-23 controller investigation, retained in
`work/kappa-publication-closeout-20261024`. That model used independent
private stages. This test uses a common stage and the full polynomial word.

`rational_cyclic_search.py` tests a signed skew simplex in characteristic
zero. It allows symplectic and general compatible rank-two projectors.
Even denominators remain allowed. Least-squares residuals are not exact
endpoints. Small residuals with growing coefficients may be degenerations.

`joint_cyclic_search.py` also varies the query maps. Its four-field control
reaches the D^3 Q endpoint numerically. No rational reconstruction is claimed.
The six-field balanced test and four-field uniform-D^2 test do not close in
their retained runs. For an order-four stage, D^3 has Gaussian rank8, not24.
The formal-stage rank25 price requires a full Laurent identity before stage
wraparound. An identity only modulo D^4-1 cannot use that price.

`extension_gadget_search.py` tests a three-call replacement for one call that
would add two D^2 Q fields. Its fixed-query real searches do not find a word.
`cyclic_stage_search.py` also accepts inverse stages and explicit signed
target degrees. The four-field signed control passes exact polynomial replay.
The favorable six-field signed endpoint is a separate search, not an attained
price. The earlier formal signed census is retained predecessor work in
`shortest-controller-stage-capacity/discovery/README.md`.

## Cyclic permutation frames

`permutation_frame_search.py` uses all24 elements of the group algebra
F2[(C2)^3 semidirect C3]. Its two-field triangle control passes literal
replay. The four-field/five-call balanced test timed out after180 seconds.
The R=1 involutive quotient has a four-field solution; that is only a
quotient solution. The six-field quotient timed out after30 seconds.

`permutation_frame_numeric.py` uses all irreducible complex representations
of this group. The two-field control closes numerically. Six four-field
balanced runs have best residual norm9.426465877034 and maximum residual
1.1846232438505457. No endpoint was found. An earlier run was interrupted
to remove the redundant constant coefficient of c; its large parameter
was a coordinate defect, not a candidate. The query-only run tests a weaker
condition and gives no Gaussian target credit.

`ADVANCING-CONTRACT.md` gives the central involution S=QG, where G uses the
whole digit-column translation. It distinguishes this from a tensor power
of the one-column G. `involution_normalization.py` checks complete kernels
at widths1 through6 and all65536 eight-axis stage entries with integers.
The all-width component proof is algebraic. The actual104-port route and
whole-caller checks remain open.

The norm-six lattice package was read as an alternative. Its19648-term
factorization is predecessor work. No new side circuit or price for that
geometry was constructed here.
