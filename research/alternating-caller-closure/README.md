# All-length gate for the fixed alternating caller

The fixed alternating-interior family cannot improve selected conditional
kappa .003125804 on the PR6030 104-port source, at any even length. The
boundary stages may be forward, inverse or absent, and their compatible
projectors may couple ports. This is a scoped obstruction, not a new kappa
or a bound on all callers. The selected frontier is unchanged.

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) combines three arguments:

- Interior curvature has rank at least (n-2)q. Each full boundary can change
  it by rank at most 4q. This excludes the large lengths.
- Six exact cyclic trace certificates close the remaining equal-sign
  boundary cases. They apply to every positive finite coupled width in
  characteristic zero. The replay uses explicit commutator identities.
- A pole-rank cut handles opposite signs. Exact rational price upper bounds
  reject these cases and all nine short-word boundary-count cases. The
  closest short bound is below -8.437 at the selected exponent.

Zero, one or two full boundary stages are allowed. The algebraic exclusions
hold at every coupled width. The price cases use the selected 104-port
invoice, or whole copies of it. All helper credits are retained and unknown
extra costs are omitted in favor of the proposed caller. The source word,
native implementation, compiler and outer theorem are not rerun here.

The gate excludes a constant monomial endpoint with the explicit fixed
interior. It does not cover changed interiors, extra queries, initialized
work, extra stage maps, noncentral coefficients or cyclic endpoints that do
not lift to a formal stage. The extra-stage rank lower bound in the proof
is not a cost bound by itself.
